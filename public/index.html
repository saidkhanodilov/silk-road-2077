COMPLETE MISSION 02 · Oct 2026 · Simulation

SimulateUz: 3D satellite launch simulator

Flies a rocket from the Baikonur launch pad to a 400 km orbit and deploys a satellite, rendered live in 3D.

  • C++17 physics engine with J2 gravity, atmospheric drag, pressure-dependent thrust, staging and fixed-step RK4 integration.
  • Closed-loop ascent guidance and a Hohmann transfer that reaches a 398 × 404 km, 51.61° orbit against a 400 km, 51.6° target, checked by an automated test that flies the full mission.
  • Real-time OpenGL 3.3 rendering in Java (LWJGL) calling the C++ core through JNI, with up to 1000× time warp and a logarithmic depth buffer from 0.5 m to 10 million km.
  • Ported to a three.js browser game, Road to Orbit, with 5 missions and an autopilot verified by headless Node.js tests.
  • C++17
